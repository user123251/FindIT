How it works

  1. Post it

    Describe what you lost or found, where, and when. Add a photo if you have one.

  2. Match it

    Search the board. Found items and lost reports sit side by side, so matches are easy to spot.

  3. Hand it back

    Message the poster, confirm a detail only the owner knows, and meet somewhere public on campus.

Stay safe: meet in busy places like the library desk or the security office. Never share passwords, card numbers or your home address. Valuables such as IDs and phones can also be handed to Campus Security.
