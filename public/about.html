Safety & FAQ

FindIt is run by students. Follow these steps so every handover goes smoothly.

Handing over an item: meet in a busy campus place, ask the owner to describe something only they would know, and bring a friend if you can. Valuables such as phones, wallets and IDs can be left with Campus Security.

How do I get my item back?

Search the board, open the matching item and send a message to the finder. Describe a detail that isn't in the post, then agree a public place to meet. Mark the item as recovered afterwards.

What should I leave out when posting a found item?

Keep one identifying detail to yourself, such as a name on the ID, a wallpaper or a serial number. Ask the owner to tell you, so you know it is really theirs.

Do I need an account?

Anyone can browse. You need an account to post, message and manage your items, which keeps the board accountable.

Who can see my contact details?

Posts show only your first name. Contact details you type into a message go to the person you are messaging and nobody else. Don't put phone numbers or addresses in public descriptions.

I found something valuable. What should I do?

Post it here, then hand it to Campus Security or the department office. Mention in the post where it is being kept.

How do I report a suspicious post?

Contact Campus Security or the student affairs office with the item name and date, and they will review it.
